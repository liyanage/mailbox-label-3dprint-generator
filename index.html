3D Printing Guidance +

Use PETG filament.

Recommended nozzles: 0.4 mm for the black base and 0.25 mm for the white lettering.

Prefer fonts with rounded shapes. They tend to print more cleanly than fonts with sharp corners.

In your slicer, configure a color change at 2 mm, switching to white for the first text layer above the black base.

For experimental 3MF, assign black filament to the base and white to the text parts. How the Text group appears depends on your slicer.
